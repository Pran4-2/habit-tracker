# 📖 Habit Tracker - Quick Start Guide

Welcome to your new Habit Tracker! This guide will help you get started in just a few minutes.

## 🚀 Getting Started (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Open the Dashboard
1. Find the file named `index.html` in this folder
2. Double-click it to open in your web browser
3. That's it! No installation needed.

### Step 2: Understand Your Dashboard

When you open the dashboard, you'll see:

**Top Row - Your Stats:**
- **Today's Progress**: Shows how much you've completed today (0-100%)
- **Current Streak**: How many days in a row you've been consistent
- **Weekly Score**: Your total XP for this week
- **Monthly Score**: Your total XP for this month

**Progress Bar:**
- Shows visual progress for today
- Fills up as you complete habits
- Displays your XP earned vs. possible

**Today's Habits:**
- List of all your habits for the day
- Click the checkbox when you complete a habit
- Each habit shows its XP value (points)
- Use Edit button to change habit details
- Use Delete button to remove habits

**Settings:**
- Toggle to include weekends in weekly score

### Step 3: Start Tracking!

**Your First Day:**
1. Look at the 3 example habits already there
2. Check off any habit you complete
3. Watch your progress bar fill up!
4. See your XP increase

**Add Your Own Habits:**
1. Click the "+ Add Habit" button
2. Enter a habit name (e.g., "Meditate 10 minutes")
3. Set XP points (1-100, based on difficulty)
4. Click Save
5. Your new habit appears in the list!

## 💡 Understanding XP (Experience Points)

Think of XP like video game points - harder tasks = more points!

**How to Assign XP:**
- **5-10 XP**: Quick, easy habits
  - Example: Make your bed, drink water
- **10-20 XP**: Regular daily tasks
  - Example: 15 min exercise, read 10 pages
- **20-50 XP**: Challenging activities
  - Example: 1 hour study session, gym workout
- **50-100 XP**: Major accomplishments
  - Example: Complete a project, run 5K

**Why XP Matters:**
- Your daily progress is calculated by XP, not number of habits
- A day with 80% XP completion counts toward your streak
- Higher XP for harder tasks motivates you to tackle them

## 📊 Scoring System Explained

### Daily Score
- **Goal**: Complete all habits (100%)
- **Progress Bar**: Visual representation of completion
- **XP Display**: Shows "Earned XP / Total Possible XP"

### Weekly Score
- **Default**: Counts Monday through Friday
- **With Weekends**: Toggle setting to include Sat/Sun
- **Resets**: Every Monday at midnight

### Monthly Score  
- **Total**: Sum of all XP earned this month
- **Resets**: First day of each month

### Streak Counter
- **What it counts**: Consecutive days with 80%+ completion
- **How it breaks**: Miss a day or earn less than 80% XP
- **Why 80%?**: Allows flexibility while maintaining consistency

## ⚙️ Managing Your Habits

### Add a New Habit
1. Click "+ Add Habit"
2. Enter habit name
3. Set XP value (1-100)
4. Click Save

### Edit a Habit
1. Click "Edit" next to the habit
2. Change name or XP value
3. Click Save

### Delete a Habit
1. Click "Delete" next to the habit
2. Confirm deletion
3. Habit and all its history are removed

## 📱 Tips for Success

### Best Practices
1. **Start Small**: Begin with 3-5 habits, not 20
2. **Be Honest**: Only check off truly completed habits
3. **Daily Routine**: Check the dashboard morning and evening
4. **Adjust XP**: If a habit feels too easy/hard, edit the XP
5. **Build Streaks**: Aim for 80%+ daily to maintain your streak

### Common Questions

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**
A: Your streak resets to 0, but your monthly score keeps accumulating. Just start a new streak tomorrow!

**Q: Can I have different habits on different days?**
A: Currently, all habits show every day. Just skip the ones that don't apply by leaving them unchecked.

**Q: Will I lose my data?**
A: Data is saved in your browser. It stays unless you clear browser data. Use the same browser to keep your data.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes! Open `index.html` on your phone's browser. Works great on mobile.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**
A: Take screenshots periodically, or use your browser's export tools to save localStorage data.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Dashboard won't open:**
- Make sure you're opening `index.html`
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- Check that JavaScript is enabled

**Data disappeared:**
- Did you clear browser data?
- Are you using the same browser?
- Check if you're in private/incognito mode

**Progress not updating:**
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Check browser console for errors
- Try clearing cache and reloading

**Checkbox won't check:**
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled
